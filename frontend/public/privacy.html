What FreakCode is

FreakCode is a free study tracker for placement interview preparation (DSA, OOPS, OS and CN). This page explains what we store about you and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username, email address and a securely hashed password (we never store or see your actual password).
  • Sign in with Google: if you use it, we receive your email address, whether Google has verified it, your name and a Google account ID. We use these only to sign you in and link your account. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or anything else.
  • Your study activity: which questions you tick as Solved, Read or Revised and when, your revision schedule, and any private notes you write.
  • Settings: your chosen username and whether you appear on the leaderboard.

How we use it

  • To run your account: logging in, saving progress, the revision queue, heatmap and streaks.
  • To send one-time codes for email verification and password reset.
  • To show the public leaderboard: only your username, points, number of questions completed and streak, and only if you leave "Show me on the leaderboard" switched on in Settings. Your email and notes are never shown to other users.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the services that run the app (below).

Services we use

  • Vercel hosts the website, Render runs the server and Neon stores the database.
  • Brevo delivers our verification and password-reset emails.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" if you choose to use it.

Who can see your data

Your notes and email are private to you. The app's administrators can see account details needed to run the service (username, email, role, sign-up date and progress totals).

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. Sign-ups that never verify their email are deleted automatically after 24 hours. To delete your account and all its data, email us from your account's address and we will remove it.

Security

Passwords are hashed with BCrypt, connections use HTTPS, and sessions use signed tokens that are revoked when you log out or log in elsewhere.
